# The AI CEO

Distilled from docs/brief.md (the authoritative build brief). Do not invent beyond it.

## Register

brand

## Product purpose

Launch site for The AI CEO by Azen AI Ltd: a free "second brain" session plus a £997 four-week cohort (10 seats, doors open 20 Aug 2026) where business owners build systems they own outright: a second brain, a speed to lead agent, a reactivation agent, and an AI CEO orchestrator that runs them all. Also a private 1-on-1 build tier (£2,500 to £3,500). (Amended 17 Aug: no deputy, no 7am brief; "agents" is allowed vocabulary.)

## Users

Owners of live small businesses in the UK. Real enquiries, real client lists, everything important living in the owner's head. They attend personally. Not AI hobbyists, not pre-revenue founders, not delegating managers.

## Brand and tone

- Voice (resolved 17 Aug): the OWNER becomes an AI-powered CEO; the software they build is THE AI CEO OS. The person is always the CEO; the OS runs the systems for them. Never present the software as replacing the owner.
- British spelling. No em-dashes. Sentence-case headings with full stops.
- Banned vocabulary (see scripts/copy-lint.sh): help, adopt, scale, leverage, automations, AI-powered, digital transformation, workforce, agents, empire, unlock, 10x, co-pilot as a name, AI Operating System.
- Concrete over abstract: times, prices, days of the week, marked exams.

## Design language

Clone of thefounderos.com's utilitarian terminal-editorial aesthetic with one systematic change: their red #EF4444 becomes AI CEO blue #1D6BFF everywhere. Six signature motifs carry the look: grid-paper backgrounds, sticky terminal HUD with per-section exec labels and scroll percentage, dither/halftone pixel art of the arrow mark, accent announcement bar, near-black inset demo windows, dotted-underline and soft-pill prose highlights.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS gradient heroes, glassmorphism, purple-on-white AI aesthetics, bouncy animation, stock icon card grids. Anything that could not be mistaken for a terminal-flavoured editorial print sheet.

## Strategic principles

- The live demo IS the proof: a real second brain runs on the page.
- The exam guarantee (16/20 or free until it passes) is the differentiator; it gets its own section.
- Performance is part of the pitch: the site about owned systems must itself feel engineered (Lighthouse 95+, no heavy libraries outside the isolated demo iframe).
